## Limits

These are packaging checks and synthetic assistant responses, not a security certification or a measured usability study. They do not establish that every model will follow the guide. No real accounts were changed, no credentials were collected, no provider recovery flow was exercised, and no partner was contacted.

The guide was not uploaded to consumer Claude/ChatGPT, installed into the person's normal Claude Code/Codex profiles, or tested with nontechnical participants. Only isolated Codex skill runs were completed; Claude Code's attempted isolated run was blocked. Those host and usability tests remain release gates. There is no live one-click website, public installer, scheduled reminder service or account-security scanner in this package.

The cited research is source-by-source web review rather than a systematic review. Australian primary-page retrieval failed; country-specific coverage is incomplete. Vendor offerings are their own documented claims, not independent audits or partnership commitments. Public licensing and a distribution home remain undecided.
